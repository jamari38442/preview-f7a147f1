What We Do

Training built around your dog

Every dog and household is different. We start with an honest evaluation, then match you to the right program, from foundational manners to specialized protection work.

Board & Train

An immersive one to three week stay where your dog lives and learns with us. They come home with real obedience habits, and we coach you through the handoff so the results stick. One, two and three week options.

Private Sessions

One-on-one training in your home or in public settings, tailored to your goals and your dog’s pace. You are hands-on from day one.

Basic & Advanced Obedience

Sit, place, recall and loose-leash walking, all the way to reliable off-leash control in real-world distractions.

Behavior Modification

For reactivity, leash aggression, anxiety and impulse control. We address the root of the behavior, not just the symptom.

Puppy Development & CGC

Socialization, foundations and AKC Canine Good Citizen preparation to start young dogs off right, from AKC evaluators.

Personal Protection & Bite Work

Confidence-based protection training for the right dogs, always built on a foundation of rock-solid obedience first.

Service Dog Task Training

Specialized task work for owners who need a purpose-trained partner for daily life, built around your specific needs.

Group Classes

Questions

Answers before you book

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Coconut Creek and train throughout Broward and Palm Beach County, including Boca Raton, Margate, Pompano Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Parkland, Tamarac and Coral Springs. In-home, public setting and board & train options are available.

How old does my puppy need to be to start?

You can start foundations and socialization young. Early, positive exposure and clear structure make every later stage of training easier, so there is rarely a reason to wait.

What is the difference between board & train and private lessons?

Board & train is an immersive one to three week stay where we do the heavy lifting and then coach you through the handoff. Private lessons keep your dog at home while we train you and your dog together each session. Both end with you in control.

Do you use e-collars? Is your method harsh?

We use a balanced approach: clear rewards and clear boundaries. Tools like food, harnesses, flat collars and e-collars are matched to the individual dog and introduced fairly and gradually, never as punishment.

Can you help with reactivity or aggression?

Yes. Behavior modification is a core part of what we do, including leash reactivity, impulse control and comfort-based behaviors. It starts with an honest evaluation so we can build the right plan.

Is protection training right for my dog?

Protection and bite work is only appropriate for the right temperament and always built on a foundation of solid obedience. We will tell you honestly during your evaluation whether it is a good fit.

How do I get started?

Book a free evaluation. We will talk through your goals, assess your dog and recommend the program that fits. There is no cost and no obligation.

What should I expect at the free evaluation?

A relaxed, hands-on assessment of your dog's temperament and behavior, a talk through your goals, and a clear, honest recommendation for the right program. No cost and no pressure to book.
